Backed By Science

How Pumpkin Seed Regrowth Oil Works

5 research-backed ways it targets why women’s hair thins after 35. No drugs, no hormones, just pumpkin seed oil and caffeine.

  1. 1

    Blocks DHT At The Follicle

    Pumpkin seed oil is rich in phytosterols shown to inhibit 5-alpha-reductase, the enzyme that turns testosterone into DHT: the hormone that shrinks follicles in female pattern hair loss.

  2. 2

    Wakes Up Dormant Follicles

    Our Caffeine Complex counteracts DHT’s suppression of follicle growth and has been shown to prolong the anagen (growth) phase in female hair follicles.

  3. 3

    Boosts Blood Flow To The Root

    Caffeine plus a 60-second scalp massage stimulates microcirculation, so oxygen and nutrients reach the follicles that have been starved of them.

  4. 4

    Strengthens Every Strand

    Omega-3, 6 and 9 fatty acids, zinc and vitamin E nourish the hair shaft and cut breakage, so the hair you grow is hair you keep.

  5. 5

    Calms An Irritated Scalp

    Anti-inflammatory tocopherols and carotenoids soothe itching, dryness and inflammation, giving follicles the healthy environment they need to grow.

Whatever Kind of Hair Loss You’re Facing, Start Here

1. HORMONAL THINNING (FEMALE PATTERN HAIR LOSS)

Perimenopause, menopause, PCOS, postpartum, coming off the pill: when oestrogen drops, DHT gets the upper hand and the follicles along your part and crown slowly shrink. Pumpkin seed oil’s DHT-blocking sterols target this directly.

2. SHEDDING FROM STRESS, ILLNESS OR RAPID WEIGHT LOSS

Telogen effluvium pushes follicles into their resting phase all at once: handfuls in the shower, a thinner ponytail. Caffeine helps pull follicles back into their growth phase so the shedding slows and regrowth can start.

Years of tight braids, weaves, buns and heat wear down the edges and temples. Autoimmune alopecia leaves patches. Elaren nourishes and calms the scalp so it can support regrowth, and works alongside any treatment your doctor recommends.
